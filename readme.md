a simple website that fetches the list of top 250 movies from IMDB, and allow you to create yout top 5,10 , 20 movies list and share it with your friends.
By drag and drop in each list, you can change the order of movies in your list, and also you can remove movies from your list by dragging them to the trash bin.
You can also search for movies in the top 250 list and add them to your list by clicking on the add button.
